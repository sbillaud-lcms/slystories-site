SLY STORIES

History's strangest secrets, in 40 seconds.

Sly Stories is an English-language YouTube Shorts channel about weird but true history: forgotten trials, odd inventions, unexpected consequences. Every episode is short, sourced and honest: no legends presented as facts, no misleading titles.

How episodes are made

  • Each script is researched, then checked claim by claim against reputable sources. The sources are listed in every video description.
  • Illustrations come from Wikimedia Commons and are limited to public-domain or openly licensed works, credited in the description.
  • The narration uses a synthetic voice, and videos are declared as containing synthetic media on YouTube.
  • A human reviews and approves every video before it is published.

Sly Stories uploader

"Sly Stories uploader" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to schedule the channel's own videos on YouTube and to read the channel's own statistics, through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to other users.
